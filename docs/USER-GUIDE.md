# PetraPMS — User guide (front office, housekeeping, maintenance)

**Shortcuts:** Ctrl+K search anywhere · Esc close dialogs. Language/digits/theme/text size: click your initial (top right) → *My preferences*.

## Reservations
*Reservations → New*: search or create the guest (duplicates are suggested), choose dates, room type(s) and rate plan; the price (incl. VAT/SC) is shown live. Add deposit, company billing, special requests. Statuses: Tentative · Confirmed · Waitlist · In house · Checked out · Cancelled · No-show. Cancelling applies the cancellation policy fee (waiving needs a manager).
The **Tape chart** shows all rooms by date: drag a booking to another room/date, double-click an empty cell to book, drag from the *Unassigned* list to give a room. In-house guests are moved with *Room move*.

## Front desk
Tabs: Arrivals · In house · Departures. **Check in** (ID details, photo, room, deposit, registration card) — foreign guests need passport/visa data for the police report. **Check out** shows the folio, takes payment or bills the company (city ledger), prints the invoice. Shift notes carry messages to the next shift.

## Folio & billing
Post charges (item codes), take payments (cash, card, bKash/Nagad/Rocket, bank), split folios (e.g. room to company, extras to guest), transfer charges, void with reason, print receipts (80 mm) and A4 invoices. Restaurant/POS charges arrive automatically. Allowances/discounts above your limit ask a manager.

## Housekeeping
Room board (tap to cycle Dirty → Clean → Inspected), daily task generation, assign attendants, supervisor inspection, lost & found, linen par levels. Attendants see **My tasks** on a phone/tablet.

## Maintenance
Raise tickets (category, priority, photo). Critical tickets can block the room automatically. Preventive schedules create tickets when due.

## Reports
17 reports incl. revenue, occupancy, ADR/RevPAR, payments, VAT/service charge, city-ledger aging, police/SB register, audit trail. View on screen, export Excel/CSV/PDF.

## Tips
Always sign out or lock when leaving the desk. Never share your PIN. If the red *connection lost* bar appears, wait for it to clear before saving.
