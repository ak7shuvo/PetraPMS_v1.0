# PetraPMS — Licence terms (template)

> Template for the vendor to review with a lawyer before sale. Replace the bracketed items.

1. **Grant.** [Vendor] grants the customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use PetraPMS at the hotel named on the licence key, up to the room and terminal counts and modules stated on it.
2. **Term.** Perpetual or term-limited as stated on the key. Support & updates are included until the *support-until* date.
3. **Trial.** A 14-day trial starts at first launch. Afterwards the software is read-only until a licence key is entered. Customer data always remains viewable and exportable.
4. **Restrictions.** No copying for other hotels, reverse engineering of the licensing mechanism, removal of licence checks, or resale, except where mandatory law allows.
5. **Data.** Customer data stays on the customer's systems. PetraPMS sends **no telemetry**. The customer is responsible for backups and for lawful handling of guest data (including NID/passport copies) under Bangladesh law.
6. **Warranty & liability.** Provided "as is" to the extent permitted by law; liability limited to the fees paid in the last 12 months. Tax features follow common practice and do not replace professional tax advice.
7. **Third-party software.** Includes open-source components under their own licences (e.g. JetBrains Mono and Noto Sans Bengali under the SIL OFL; licence texts are included in the installation).
8. **Governing law.** [Laws of Bangladesh; courts of Dhaka.]
